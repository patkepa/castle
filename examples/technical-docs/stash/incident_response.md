---
title: Incident response
---

Use this process when a Halcyon service threatens customer impact or a reliability objective.

## First ten minutes

1. Declare an incident and assign an incident commander.
2. Open the incident channel and shared timeline.
3. State the observed impact and the current working hypothesis.
4. Prefer a safe mitigation over a complete diagnosis.

## Stabilize the system

Use feature controls, traffic shaping, regional isolation, or rollback to reduce impact. Avoid simultaneous speculative changes. Announce each action before execution and record its result.

## Recovery and follow-up

Verify recovery using customer-facing indicators, not only internal health checks. Schedule a blameless review and track corrective work to completion.

> This is demonstration content for the fictional Halcyon Systems company, not a production incident policy.
