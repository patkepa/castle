Start here to understand how Halcyon engineers design, ship, and operate the platform.

## Using this handbook

The handbook is our durable technical memory. Prefer concise documents with a clear owner, concrete examples, and links to the systems they describe.

For the big picture, read [platform architecture](../wiki/platform_architecture.md). Before opening a change, follow [local development](../wiki/local_development.md).

## Where knowledge belongs

- Platform documents explain shared architecture and engineering standards.
- Service documents describe ownership, interfaces, and operational boundaries.
- Operations documents contain deployment and incident procedures.

> Halcyon Systems is fictional. This handbook contains demonstration data only.
