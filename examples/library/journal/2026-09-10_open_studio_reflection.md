# Open studio reflection

The most useful feedback at the [[events/2026/atlas_open_studio|Atlas open
studio]] was a request for more uncertainty. Visitors wanted to know whether a
place was welcoming *for them*, not receive a universal verdict.

Maya noticed that the large-text labels helped people approach the display at
their own speed. Sofia suggested carrying that same low-pressure feeling into
the [[events/2026/community_listening_session|community listening session]].

## A small revision for the next draft

Replace “best places” with “places people return to,” and attach a short reason
only when it is shareable. The revision follows [[notes/consent_and_care|the
consent guidance]] and gives the team a concrete prompt for
[[tasks/synthesize_interviews|interview synthesis]].
