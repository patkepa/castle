# Field day — harbor to pocket garden

Alex, Jamie, and [[people/sofia_rossi|Sofia]] walked from the harbor steps to
the pocket garden using the [[notes/interview_kit|conversation kit]].

![Illustration of a quiet pocket garden with tall grasses, a curved bench, and a neighborhood path](assets/images/pocket-garden-field-day.png)

*A fictional field-day scene. The goal is to notice the conditions for welcome,
not to rate a place or its visitors.*

## 09:10 — Harbor steps

The strongest signal was not the view but the movable chairs. People made
temporary circles, then returned the chairs to the wall. A tiny ritual of care.

## 10:25 — Bakery window

A water bowl and a hand-drawn notice created more conversation than the polished
signage. This supports the “invite, do not rank” principle in
[[notes/design_principles|Design principles]].

## 11:40 — Pocket garden

There was a genuine quiet corner behind the tall grasses. Sofia noted that the
garden feels social without requiring participation—a useful nuance for
[[notes/research/third_places|third-place research]].
