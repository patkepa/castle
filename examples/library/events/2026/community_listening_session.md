# Community listening session

Amara will facilitate a small table conversation at Harbor Commons. The team
will bring anonymous summaries from the field day and open studio, explain the
sharing levels in [[notes/consent_and_care|Consent and care for field notes]],
and ask what feels missing from the first Atlas draft.

Bring the [[tasks/prepare_listening_session|listening-session materials]] and
leave room for feedback that does not fit a map.
