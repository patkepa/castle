# Graph field guide

Castle builds its relationship graph from the frontmatter of notes in `people/`.
This example library intentionally includes several shapes.

## What to look for

- **Relation colors:** the sample covers positive, neutral, flirty, mixed, and
  negative connections—from close collaborators to former coworkers.
- **Known-from branches:** expand Community, Work, Conference, University, and Family.
- **Organizations:** Northstar Labs has Research, Design, and Engineering departments;
  Harbor Commons has Programs and Partnerships.
- **Direct links:** dotted connections describe coworkers, friends, family, classmates,
  and manager relationships.
- **Map locations:** several people have coordinates; Jamie demonstrates multiple locations.
