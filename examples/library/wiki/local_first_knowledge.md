# Local-first knowledge

Local-first tools keep the durable source close to the person doing the work.
In Castle, Markdown files are authoritative; generated graphs, search indexes,
and databases are replaceable views.

## Practical consequences

1. Notes remain readable without the application.
2. Links such as [[projects/neighborhood_atlas/research_plan|the research plan]]
   remain explicit in source.
3. Sensitive raw interviews can stay outside publishable summaries.
4. A disposable index can make search fast without becoming a second source of truth.

Noah applies these ideas to the
[[projects/neighborhood_atlas/data_dictionary|Atlas data dictionary]], especially
the consent and evidence fields.
