# Welcome to the Neighborhood Atlas

This publish-safe library follows a fictional group building a living guide to
their neighborhood. It is deliberately connected: people know one another,
projects create tasks and events, journal entries point back to decisions, and
the same ideas appear in the Canvas and spreadsheet workspaces.

> Start with the [[wiki/castle_feature_tour|Castle feature tour]], then open the
> relationship graph and follow any person or note link that catches your eye.

## The story so far

[[people/alex_morgan|Alex Morgan]] and [[people/jamie_chen|Jamie Chen]] began by
mapping the places that make a neighborhood feel welcoming. [[people/maya_singh|Maya Singh]]
turned the research into a visual language, while [[people/noah_williams|Noah Williams]]
built a lightweight data pipeline. Community hosts [[people/sofia_rossi|Sofia Rossi]]
and [[people/amara_okafor|Amara Okafor]] keep the work grounded in real conversations.

Their current focus is the [[projects/neighborhood_atlas/neighborhood_atlas|Neighborhood Atlas]].
The earlier [[projects/listening_garden/listening_garden|Listening Garden]] is complete,
and the [[projects/mobile_story_booth/mobile_story_booth|Mobile Story Booth]] is waiting
for its next planning window.

After the open studio, the group is preparing a
[[events/2026/community_listening_session|community listening session]] to decide
which observations should shape the next public draft.
