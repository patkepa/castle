# Why third places matter

“Third places” are informal gathering spaces outside home and work. For the
Atlas, the useful idea is not a strict category but a set of qualities:
low barriers to entry, regulars and newcomers sharing space, and reasons to
linger without a formal appointment.

## Signals for fieldwork

| Signal | Observation prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome | Can a newcomer understand how to join? |
| Rhythm | Are there recurring rituals or familiar faces? |
| Choice | Can someone be social or find a quiet corner? |
| Care | Is maintenance visible as a shared practice? |

These signals inform the [[notes/interview_kit|conversation kit]], but they are
not scores. That distinction follows [[notes/design_principles|Design principles]].
