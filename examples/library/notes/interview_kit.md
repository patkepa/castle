# Conversation kit

Use these prompts during short walking conversations for the
[[projects/neighborhood_atlas/neighborhood_atlas|Neighborhood Atlas]].

## Opening

> Show me a place where you naturally slow down.

Ask permission before taking notes. Explain whether the result is private,
summarized, or publishable.

## Prompts

- What makes this place easy—or difficult—to enter?
- When does it feel most alive?
- Is there a ritual a newcomer might not notice?
- Where is the quiet corner?
- What would you never want a map to flatten into a rating?

## Closing checklist

- [ ] Reflect one thing you heard
- [ ] Confirm the consent level
- [ ] Offer a way to revise the summary
- [ ] Add only the necessary fields to the [[projects/neighborhood_atlas/data_dictionary|data dictionary]]
