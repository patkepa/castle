# Consent and care for field notes

The [[projects/neighborhood_atlas/neighborhood_atlas|Neighborhood Atlas]] is a
record of invitations, not a directory of people. Every observation should leave
someone with more control than they had before the conversation began.

## Three sharing levels

| Level | What we keep | Where it can appear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private | Full context and contact details | The participant's own follow-up only |
| Team summary | A paraphrased observation without identifiers | Research synthesis |
| Shareable story | Participant-approved language and attribution | Atlas prototype or event display |

If a participant changes their mind, remove the observation from the next
working draft and confirm the change. Do not infer a sharing level from an
enthusiastic conversation.

## Before and after a conversation

- [ ] Use the [[notes/interview_kit|conversation kit]] to explain the purpose.
- [ ] Ask which sharing level feels right today.
- [ ] Write observations rather than judgments.
- [ ] Send a short summary when a follow-up route was requested.
- [ ] Bring any unresolved questions to the [[events/2026/community_listening_session|community listening session]].

This is the practical companion to the “invite, do not rank” principle in
[[notes/design_principles|Design principles]].
