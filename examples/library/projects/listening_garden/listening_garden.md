# Listening Garden

A completed weekend installation where visitors left short voice notes about
places they cared for. Sofia hosted the garden and Leo assembled a gentle
soundscape from opt-in recordings.

## What carried forward

The project proved that a contribution can be meaningful without being long.
That lesson now shapes the [[projects/neighborhood_atlas/neighborhood_atlas|Neighborhood Atlas]]
and the proposed [[projects/mobile_story_booth/mobile_story_booth|Mobile Story Booth]].
