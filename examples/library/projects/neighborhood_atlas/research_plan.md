# Atlas research plan

## Questions

1. Where do people linger without needing to buy something?
2. Which small rituals make a place feel cared for?
3. What information helps a newcomer participate?

## Method

Run eight short walking conversations using the
[[notes/interview_kit|conversation kit]]. Pair each interview with a sketch,
one memorable quote, and a consent level. Jamie leads the interviews, Alex
facilitates synthesis, and Maya tests the visual vocabulary.

See [[projects/neighborhood_atlas/data_dictionary|Data dictionary]] for the
fields and [[wiki/local_first_knowledge|Local-first knowledge]] for why raw
interview material stays on the researcher's device.
