# Mobile Story Booth

A lightweight recording booth that can travel between community events. The
plan borrows the welcoming format of the
[[projects/listening_garden/listening_garden|Listening Garden]] and the consent
model from the [[projects/neighborhood_atlas/neighborhood_atlas|Neighborhood Atlas]].

The project remains planned until funding and accessibility review are complete.
